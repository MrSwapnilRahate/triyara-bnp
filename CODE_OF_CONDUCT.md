# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of background or identity, and to act in ways that foster an open, welcoming,
and professional community.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behaviour: empathy and kindness, respect for differing opinions,
constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the
project and its users.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or
political attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to `conduct@triyaraexports.com`. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are
responsible for clarifying standards and may take appropriate corrective action.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
